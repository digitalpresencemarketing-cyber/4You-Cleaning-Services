    Good questions

    Frequently asked questions

    How does the instant estimate work?

    Tell us about your space, the service you need and how often. Our estimator gives you an approximate price range right away, and our team confirms the final price with you before any cleaning is scheduled.

    What areas do you serve?

    We serve towns across the South Shore and Cape Cod, Massachusetts, from Quincy and Hingham down to Plymouth, Falmouth, Hyannis, Chatham and Provincetown. See the full map.

    What are your hours?

    We clean Monday through Saturday, from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

    Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

    Not at all. Many clients share a key, lockbox or door code. Just let us know your preference when we confirm your booking.

    Do you clean Airbnbs and vacation rentals between guests?

    Yes. We offer turnover cleaning for Airbnb and vacation rentals on the Cape and South Shore, so every guest walks into a fresh, guest-ready space.

    What if I'm not happy with the cleaning?

    That's what our Spotless Promise is for. Let us know within 24 hours and we'll come back and re-clean the area at no extra cost.
